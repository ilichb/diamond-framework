This release is an errata release, not a feature release: one label was wrong in
the deposited v0.2.2, and the paper now prints the figure the deployed system
actually pays. No contract and no test changed; the delta in `main.tex` against
the v0.2.2 deposit is **10 lines added, 9 removed**.

## What was wrong

The paper priced the deployed path at **286,144 gas**. That is the cost of V3's
**three-input** key (`[root, epoch, certifiedNorm]`). The registry installs
M4d's **four-input** key (`[root, epoch, validator, certifiedNorm]`), and a
three-input key cannot be installed at all: `Groth16Verifier.verify` requires
`publicInputs.length == ic.length - 1`, `verifyThreshold` passes four, and the
call reverts `PublicInputsMismatch(3, 4)`.

The defect was not that the paper hid the key — it says in §4 that V3 "is no
longer the admission statement the manager accepts" — but that it labelled a key
nothing installs as "the deployed figure" and then derived the break-even, the
margin and two ratios from it.

## What the paper prints now

| Quantity | Gas | Convention |
|---|---:|---|
| `verifyThreshold` cold, **installed key** (the deployed figure) | **306,253** | `M4dRealKey.t.sol`, committed test |
| `verifyThreshold` warm, installed key | **247,211** | same test |
| `verifyThreshold` cold, V3's key (history) | 286,144 | v0.2.2, retired harness |
| `manager.admit` cold, installed key, first admission | **395,255** | measured for the first time here |

286,144 stays in the paper only where the text says it is V3's. The complete
admission path had never been re-measured since the V3 era: it is 395,255 cold,
and 329,682 for a second admission, which puts the manager's own overhead at
89,002 gas (395,255 − 306,253) and makes that overhead convention-independent.
The paper prints 395,255; the other two numbers live in
[`v0.2.3-errata.md`](v0.2.3-errata.md) and in `recompute.py`'s output, not in the
manuscript.

The paper prints these at its own precision (`730` gwei, `2.2×`, `21×`,
`29 times`, `$0.00766$`, `566` gwei) and puts V3's own figure beside the
comparison wherever the comparison needs it (`781` gwei, `594` gwei, `286,144`).
Two ratios that hang off the admission cost are **not** printed: `1.82×` with
376,588 and `1.73×` with 395,255 — both are computed in `recompute.py` and stated

**No conclusion of the paper changes.** Verification stays more than two orders
of magnitude below the ingest, Bulletproofs stays roughly an order above it, and
the break-even stays far above any realistic fee. The labels changed, and every
number derived from them moved with them: the seven quantities in the table
above.

## Two numbers a re-measurer will hit

They are stated here so that neither looks like a discrepancy:

- **306,275** is the same quantity priced on the preceding toolchain (M4d,
  implementation plan §5.5.11), 22 gas above 306,253; the
  [evidence README](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/README.md)
  records that figure so the 22 gas cannot be mistaken for a finding. The paper
  prints 306,253 and keeps 306,275 in parentheses.
- **35 gas of the 306,253 is the harness.** `M4dRealKey.t.sol` builds the proof
  blob through an internal helper inside the measured window; with the blob
  inline the same call is 306,218 cold / 247,176 warm. V3's 223,137 is likewise
  266 gas below the 223,403 the paper printed for it: same quantity, older
  toolchain.

## What a reader must not conclude

- **Not that the deployed system became ~7% more expensive recently.** 306,253 is
  not "V3 plus a fix". It is a key with one more public input, on a registry that
  also grew (`Bn254.decodeProof`'s coordinate validation, the `MAX_NORM` domain
  check). Of the 20,109 gas between the two figures, 8,873 is the extra `ic`
  scalar multiplication; the registry's share is left undecomposed rather than
  attributed to the key.
- **Not that 376,588 is comparable with 395,255.** The difference is 18,667, and
  the manager changed as well, so the missing 1,442 gas is not the key's either.
  The V3-era probe that produced 376,588 was deleted; the entry states the
  measured total and refuses the decomposition it cannot support.
- **Not that "deployed" means "certified".** The verifier is deployed from a
  development key; no ceremony-key verifier exists. That limit is unchanged by
  this release.
