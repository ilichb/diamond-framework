## Errata (2026-10-20, after publication)

The PDF in this release is unchanged. Two statements in it are corrected here so
that the release is not cited as implemented fact:

- **Validator binding (§6.2).** The sentence "the proof binds the admission to a
  specific committed tensor, and the manager ties the admission call to the
  validator" does not establish that the proof is bound to the validator. The
  statement verified by `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold` is
  `[root, epoch, certifiedNorm]`; `AVIPValidatorManager.admit` ties the *call*
  to `msg.sender`, but the same proof is accepted for a different address in the
  same epoch. This is confirmed with the real Groth16 fixture (audit finding
  P-01). A validator-bound statement is designed but not implemented; do not cite
  the sentence as a property of v0.2.1. A corrected release will follow.
- **Gas figure (~206k).** That number is the composition of primitives with the
  verifying key inlined; it is not the measured cold path of
  `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold`. With the real proof, the cold path is
  **286,144 gas**, and that is the figure used for the verification-only
  break-even (**781 gwei**, **2.39x**). The full `manager.admit` path is
  **376,588 gas** (**594 gwei**, **1.82x**). The 1.59x ingest/verification
  ratio mixes compiler profiles and must not be cited until both sides use the
  same build.

## What changed

- **ZK paper ↔ code.** §4 now describes the delivered V3 Poseidon-sponge
  circuit with direct Groth16 verification: 94,438 constraints, 3.9 s
  proving, 128-byte proof (256 bytes on-chain), ~206k gas (superseded: see
  Errata). The recursive
  option is deferred, not adopted. `573` is corrected to `3,621` for the
  old Tucker reconstruction; Option B is measured at 1.22 KB and is not
  adopted.
- **Cost of Attack.** The unsupported "without degrading" claim is
  removed. §1/§6 state that the paper prices admission, not the cost of
  acquiring reputation; the open question is recorded in §10.
- **Audit model.** The table is labelled comparative statics at exogenous
  `p_fraud = 0.10`, not an equilibrium. The `p_fraud → 0`, `B* → ∞` loop
  and the verifier's dilemma are stated. The publisher of a fraudulent
  leaf is the sequencer in v1; attribution to a validator is declared not
  implemented.
- **Attack model.** The worst-case inflation `r_fraud → ∞` is added:
  `q > 0.2857` at `k=1`, `q > 0.7826` at `k=9`. Freshness is stated as a
  bound on entry count, not on the norm.
- **Tensor.** The synthetic test tensors are labelled outside
  `T ∈ [0,1]^500`; the blocky explanation now attributes the 52.5% error
  to truncating modes D/H, not to block structure in mode C.
- **Bootstrap.** `0.7764`/`0.3936` are labelled as means (medians
  0.7714/0.4000; median reduction 22.86%). The bootstrap value function
  is declared a separate model choice from §2.
- **Provenance.** EIP-1108 replaces EIP-196/197; 160 wei/gas is cited as
  a Fuji testnet observation; `p=1` is defined; the Fuji scope table is
  added; the title date is October 2026.
- **Status labels.** Table 11.1: collusion is "Verified (synthetic ER);
  heavy-tailed negative"; ZK is "V3 measured; verifier not deployed";
  data layer is "Verified (conditional model)".
