## What changed in the text

- **§6 (Payment Rule), validator binding (P-01).** The sentence retired by the v0.2.1
  errata ("the manager ties the admission call to the validator") is gone. It is
  replaced by what M4d delivers: the proof opens the leaf `Poseidon(i, r)` in the
  epoch's committed root with `0 <= r < 1`, and the registry builds the
  four-word statement from its own storage with the validator address as a
  public input, so a proof that admits one address cannot be replayed for
  another. Two limits stay declared: the certificate binds an entry, not a
  validator's tenure, and the verifier is not deployed.
- **§4 (circuit options) and §9 (gas), P-25.** `~206k` is now labelled as what it is: the
  composition of primitives with the verifying key **inlined**, used to compare
  circuit options like with like. The deployed figure is the cold path of
  `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold`, **286,144 gas** (223,403 warm; the full
  `manager.admit` is 376,588). Everything derived from it moves with it:
  `g* = 781 gwei` at the cold path (594 gwei for a complete admission), the cost
  at 25 gwei is `0.00715 AVAX` and the margin is **31×**, not 43×, and the
  tensor-transmission comparison is **2.4×** the verification cost, not 3.3×.
  The two scopes are never mixed, and the 1.59× ratio is still not cited.
- **§5 (Collusion Detection) and §10, P-27.** §5 now opens by saying that `W` is an
  input to the analysis and not an output of the protocol: the detector takes the
  graph as an argument and no component derives it from `T` or from the ingestion
  of consensus events (open item D-7). §10 adds the on-chain half: the alert flag
  is written once per source-chain epoch, cannot be revoked, has no consumer, and
  moves no stake or weight — with the false-positive rate of the heavy-tailed
  null at 1.0, it is an alert, not a sanction.
- **§5 (Collusion Detection, limitations) and §6, the financial floor (P-28).** "The protocol's guarantee against a
  heavy-tailed collusive core rests on the financial floor" becomes the minimum
  admission cost: `S_f*(T) >= 0.6` over the domain the circuit certifies, with
  `S_min = 0.2Θ` as the floor of the unbounded model, one fifth of the pure-PoS
  stake per node. A price of entry, explicitly not a guarantee of security, and
  the acquisition cost of reputation is still not priced.
- **Minor corrections (P-25).** The domain of §2 states the Feller condition
  instead of imposing an absorbing boundary the process cannot reach; the
  second-regime tail is "approximately geometric" with the measured ratio
  `[0.9858, 0.9973]` beside it; the Tucker error is "proportional to, and
  systematically below" the noise-to-signal ratio (`[0.848, 0.938]`, mean
  `0.892`), not equal to it.
- **README (P-29).** "Not by trusting a committee or a centralized registry" is
  removed. A new section, *Trust assumptions in v1*, enumerates the five roles
  (`sequencer`, `guardian`, `slashAuthority`, the slashing channel's `NOTIFIER`
  and the mutable, undeployed `verifier`) and what each can do. The
  non-transferability row now states the identity-layer assumption.
