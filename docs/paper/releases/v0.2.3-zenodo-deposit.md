### Description (HTML, four paragraphs)

    <p>Proof-of-stake secures admission and deterrence with a single instrument,
    financial capital, and that is the source of its structural tension: high
    collateral requirements deter honest but capital-constrained validators, low
    requirements expose the network to Sybil attacks. The Diamond Framework
    replaces a fraction of the financial stake with verifiable, non-transferable
    reputation, so the admission barrier falls without claiming that the full
    Cost of Attack is preserved; that question is left open in the paper.</p>

    <p>This deposit is the frozen PDF snapshot of the manuscript, v0.2.3, with its
    source. v0.2.3 is an errata release and changes no contract and no test. The
    paper had printed 286,144 gas as the deployed verification cost; that figure
    belongs to V3's three-input verifying key, while the key the registry installs
    has four public inputs and measures 306,253 gas cold and 247,211 warm. The
    installed key's figure is now the deployed one (break-even 730 gwei, 29x margin
    at 25 gwei, the ingest at 2.2x the verification), and 286,144 is kept only
    where the text says it is V3's. The full admission path is measured for the
    first time: manager.admit costs 395,255 gas cold for a first admission under
    the installed key (break-even 566 gwei). No conclusion of the paper moves:
    verification stays more than two orders of magnitude below the ingest and
    Bulletproofs stays roughly an order of magnitude above it.</p>

    <p>Artifact: diamond-framework-2026-10-29.pdf, 43 pages, 592894 bytes,
    SHA-256 1468b5d744ab02d6236742cf4868377b6d6aa50464d485db24c8d6270ae478fd;
    source main.tex, 156922 bytes, SHA-256
    80a7034f2115bdcb1094685a41cbdce1bc0105c6591b82249980074cb002222a. The claim
    registry that binds each quantitative claim to the script reproducing it is
    sealed by an eleven-file anchor root
    (SHA-256 76e95f6dbc58d4341d5a7c5b24a8445241a536bdca06631197b43bae21744dbb).
    That root has no OpenTimestamps receipt yet: the receipt in the repository
    seals the preceding root (abe9c553...), continues to attest it, and is not
    cited as evidence for these bytes. The v0.2.2 record is not rewritten either;
    a metadata notice in that record points here.</p>

    <p>Verification is offline and reproducible: 316 Foundry tests (0 failed, 1
    skipped), the numerical suite (38 scripts in the manifest, byte-identical
    reruns) and an 11/11 audit gate. Limits are declared rather than omitted: the
    Groth16 verifier deployed on the Avalanche Fuji testnet uses a committed
    development key, carries no ceremony and has recorded no certified admission;
    the recursive option and Teleporter cross-subnet latency remain deferred; and
    the external audit is pending. Bitcoin enters only as an external witness of

citable statement, and "deployed" is not "certified".
