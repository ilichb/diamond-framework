## E-01 The paper calls V3's cost "the deployed" one

### What the paper says

`main.tex` prices the deployed path at **286,144 gas** and derives four figures
from it:

| Line | Text |
|---|---|
| 537 | "With the key in storage the same check costs 223,403 gas warm and 286,144 gas cold, and Section~\ref{subsec:evalgas} uses the cold path **for the deployed figure**." |
| 559 | "The V3 proof is verified directly on-chain at 286,144 gas **on the deployed cold path** (223,403 warm...)" |
| 567 | "...2.4× the cost of verifying the V3 Groth16 proof directly (286,144 gas **on the deployed cold path**)" |
| 1294 | "The V3 Groth16 proof of Section~\ref{sec:zk} verifies directly at 286,144 gas on the cold path of `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold` (223,403 warm), measured with the real proof" |
| 1303 | "For $G = 286{,}144$ gas, the cold verification path, $g^* \approx 781$~gwei ... At a realistic 25~gwei it costs $0.00715$ AVAX, still **31 times** below break-even." |
| 1349 | "Groth16 verification, cold path: $286{,}144$ gas & Measured" |

Two of those sentences carry their own qualifier ("the V3 Groth16 proof"), and
`main.tex:555` states plainly that V3 "is no longer the admission statement the
manager accepts ... the figures priced here are V3's". **The defect is therefore
not that the paper hides the key — it is that it labels a key nothing installs as
"deployed", and then derives the break-even, the margin and two ratios from it.**

### Why it is wrong

The registry installs **M4d's four-input key** (`[root, epoch, validator,
certifiedNorm]`), not V3's three-input one. `Groth16Verifier.verify` requires
`publicInputs.length == ic.length - 1`; `verifyThreshold` passes four, so a
three-input key **cannot be installed at all** — the call reverts
`PublicInputsMismatch(3, 4)`. Measured 2026-10-28
([`../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/README.md)):

| Key | Public inputs | `ic.length` | `verify` gas |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| V3 (`M4bGroth16Fixtures`) | 3 | 4 | 223,137 |
| M4d (`M4dGroth16Fixtures`) | 4 | 5 | 232,010 |

V3's 223,137 reproduces the paper's 223,403 warm to 266 gas, which is what makes
this the same measurement and not a different one.

### What the correct figure is

`verifyThreshold` cold is **306,275** with M4d's key
([`../../architecture/implementation-plan.md`](../../architecture/implementation-plan.md)
§5.5.11) and **306,253** on a 2026-10-28 re-run of the same test (22 gas lower:
toolchain). Of the 20,131-gas gap, **8,873** is the extra `ic` scalar
multiplication; the rest is `AVIPRegistry`'s own growth since 2026-10-20
(`Bn254.decodeProof`'s coordinate validation, the `MAX_NORM` domain check). The
paper's 286,144 was measured on 2026-10-20, before P-01/P-16 replaced V3.

### The figures that move

From [`recompute.py`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/recompute.py)
(output in `gas_recompute.txt`), same formula as P-25's `gas_break_even.py`:

| Quantity | With 286,144 (printed today) | With 306,275 (installed key) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Break-even gas price `g*` | 781.4 gwei | **730.1 gwei** |
| Ingest over verification (`684,064 / G`) | 2.39× | **2.23×** |
| Bulletproofs over Groth16 (`6.5M / G`) | 22.7× | **21.2×** |
| Margin at 25 gwei | 31.3× | **29.2×** |
| Cost at 25 gwei | 0.0071536 AVAX | **0.0076569 AVAX** |
| Cost at 160 wei (Fuji) | 4.58e-11 AVAX | **4.90e-11 AVAX** |

No conclusion of the paper changes: verification stays more than two orders of
magnitude below the ingest, Bulletproofs stays roughly an order above it, and the
break-even stays far above any realistic fee. The *labels* change, and one number
in a table caption does.

`manager.admit`'s **376,588** is also V3-era. It is not re-derived here — that is
a probe on the deployed manager, not arithmetic — and it must be re-measured
before v0.2.3 quotes it beside M4d's verification.

### Proposed edit, for whoever applies it

1. `main.tex:1294` — say the key: "verifies directly at 286,144 gas on the cold
   path of `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold` **with V3's three-input key**; the
   four-input key the registry installs measures 306,275 cold / 247,233 warm".
2. `main.tex:537` — "uses the cold path for the deployed figure" → "uses the cold
   path of V3's key for the figure it prices (§\ref{sec:zk})".
3. `main.tex:559`, `567` — "on the deployed cold path" → "on the cold path under
   V3's key". `567`'s `2.4×` is correct *as a comparison with V3* and can stay
   once the base says V3.
4. `main.tex:1303` — replace `781` with `730`, `31` with `29`, `0.00715` with
   `0.00766`; or keep V3's numbers and say they are V3's. Not both silently.
5. `main.tex:1349` — "286,144 gas" → "286,144 gas (V3's key) / 306,275 (installed
   key)".
6. `v0.2.2-notes.md` and `v0.2.2-zenodo-deposit.md` are **not** rewritten: they
   are the record of what v0.2.2 said. This file is where the correction lives
   until v0.2.3 carries it.

**The open decision, and it is the owner's:** keep 286,144 and relabel it as V3's,
or print the installed key's 306,275 as the deployed figure. The evidence supports
either; it is not self-evident which a reader of a security paper should be given
first. What is not defensible is the current sentence, which says "deployed" and
means "V3".
