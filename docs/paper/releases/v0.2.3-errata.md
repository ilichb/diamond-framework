## E-01 The paper calls V3's cost "the deployed" one

### What the paper says

`main.tex` prices the deployed path at **286,144 gas** and derives four figures
from it:

| Line | Text |
|---|---|
| 537 | "With the key in storage the same check costs 223,403 gas warm and 286,144 gas cold, and Section~\ref{subsec:evalgas} uses the cold path **for the deployed figure**." |
| 559 | "The V3 proof is verified directly on-chain at 286,144 gas **on the deployed cold path** (223,403 warm...)" |
| 567 | "...2.4× the cost of verifying the V3 Groth16 proof directly (286,144 gas **on the deployed cold path**)" |
| 1294 | "The V3 Groth16 proof of Section~\ref{sec:zk} verifies directly at 286,144 gas on the cold path of `AVIPRegistry.verifyThreshold` (223,403 warm), measured with the real proof" |
| 1303 | "For $G = 286{,}144$ gas, the cold verification path, $g^* \approx 781$~gwei ... At a realistic 25~gwei it costs $0.00715$ AVAX, still **31 times** below break-even." |
| 1349 | "Groth16 verification, cold path: $286{,}144$ gas & Measured" |

Two of those sentences carry their own qualifier ("the V3 Groth16 proof"), and
`main.tex:555` states plainly that V3 "is no longer the admission statement the
manager accepts ... the figures priced here are V3's". **The defect is therefore
not that the paper hides the key — it is that it labels a key nothing installs as
"deployed", and then derives the break-even, the margin and two ratios from it.**

### Why it is wrong

The registry installs **M4d's four-input key** (`[root, epoch, validator,
certifiedNorm]`), not V3's three-input one. `Groth16Verifier.verify` requires
`publicInputs.length == ic.length - 1`; `verifyThreshold` passes four, so a
three-input key **cannot be installed at all** — the call reverts
`PublicInputsMismatch(3, 4)`. Measured 2026-10-28
([`../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/README.md)):

| Key | Public inputs | `ic.length` | `verify` gas |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| V3 (`M4bGroth16Fixtures`) | 3 | 4 | 223,137 |
| M4d (`M4dGroth16Fixtures`) | 4 | 5 | 232,010 |

**Provenance.** Contracts at commit `be49336` (the commit that adds this paragraph
moves only `docs/`: `git diff be49336 -- integrations/avalanche/contracts` is empty);
forge `1.5.1-stable`; solc `0.8.33`; `optimizer = false`, `evm_version = "cancun"`
from `integrations/avalanche/foundry.toml`. Convention: `gasleft()` around the
external call, so the 21,000 intrinsic and the calldata are excluded — the
convention 286,144 already used. Logs in
[`logs/`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/logs/v3_vs_m4d_key.txt).

### What v0.2.3 prints

**Decided by the owner, 2026-10-28: the deployed figure is the installed key's.**
286,144 stays in the paper only where the text says it is V3's.

| Quantity | Gas | Convention |
|---|---:|---|
| `verifyThreshold` cold, **installed key** (the deployed figure) | **306,253** | `M4dRealKey.t.sol`, committed test |
| `verifyThreshold` warm, installed key | **247,211** | same test |
| `verifyThreshold` cold, V3's key (history) | 286,144 | v0.2.2, retired harness |
| `verifyThreshold` warm, V3's key (history) | 223,403 | v0.2.2 |

306,253 is not a new measurement of a new thing: it is
[`implementation-plan.md`](../../architecture/implementation-plan.md) §5.5.11's
**306,275**, re-run on today's toolchain, 22 gas lower. Both are stated so that the
22 gas cannot be mistaken for a finding.

Two smaller numbers, because they are what a re-measurer will hit and they must not
look like discrepancies:

- **35 gas of the 306,253 is the harness, not the contract.** `M4dRealKey.t.sol`
  builds the proof blob through an internal helper *inside* the measured window; the
  same call with the blob inline is **306,218** cold / **247,176** warm. Bisected in
  [`TmpManagerAdmitM4d.t.sol`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/TmpManagerAdmitM4d.t.sol)
  (`test_mirrorWithHelper` vs `test_mirrorWithoutManager`); padding the caller's
  memory moves it 3 gas, so the residual is the helper's call, not the registry's.
- **V3's 223,137 is 266 gas below the paper's 223,403.** Same quantity, older
  toolchain; nothing follows from either beyond that.

Of the gap between the two deployed figures — **20,109** against 306,253, or
**20,131** against §5.5.11's 306,275 — **8,873** is the extra `ic` scalar
multiplication; the rest is
`AVIPRegistry`'s own growth since 2026-10-20 (`Bn254.decodeProof`'s coordinate
validation, the `MAX_NORM` domain check). The key's share is measured; the
contract's share is left undecomposed rather than attributed to the key.

### The figures that move

From [`recompute.py`](../../audit/evidence/2026-10-28-v3-vs-m4d-gas/recompute.py)
(output in `gas_recompute.txt`), same formula as P-25's `gas_break_even.py`, with
the measured 306,253 in the deployed column:

| Quantity | With 286,144 (V3, printed today) | With 306,253 (installed, v0.2.3) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Break-even gas price `g*` | 781.4 gwei | **730.1 gwei** |
| Ingest over verification (`684,064 / G`) | 2.39× | **2.23×** |
| Bulletproofs over Groth16 (`6.5M / G`) | 22.7× | **21.2×** |
| Margin at 25 gwei | 31.3× | **29.2×** |
| Cost at 25 gwei | 0.0071536 AVAX | **0.0076563 AVAX** |
| Cost at 160 wei (Fuji) | 4.58e-11 AVAX | **4.90e-11 AVAX** |

No conclusion of the paper changes: verification stays more than two orders of
magnitude below the ingest, Bulletproofs stays roughly an order above it, and the
break-even stays far above any realistic fee. The *labels* change, and four printed
numbers do.

### The admission path, measured for the first time

`manager.admit` — `AVIPValidatorManager.admit` calling `registry.verifyThreshold`
internally — was **376,588 gas** in the V3 era and had never been re-measured.
Measured now, same commit and toolchain, installed key, deterministic 500-entry
fixture:

| Path | Gas | Convention |
|---|---:|---|
| `manager.admit` cold, installed key | **395,255** | first call in the transaction, blob built like the test |
| `manager.admit` cold, helper excluded | 395,220 | blob inline |
| `manager.admit`, second admission (other validator) | 329,682 | code and epoch slots warm |
| `manager.admit` cold, V3 era (2026-10-20) | 376,588 | not comparable, see below |

The manager's own overhead is **89,002 gas** — 395,255 − 306,253, and 395,220 −
306,218 — so it is convention-independent, and `recompute.py` refuses to print if
the two ever disagree.

**376,588 is not comparable with 395,255**, and the difference is *smaller* than the
key's: 18,667 against the verification's 20,109. `AVIPValidatorManager` itself also
changed between 2026-10-20 and today (M9b/M9c, the custody handshake, on top of
`AVIPRegistry`'s growth), so the missing 1,442 gas is not attributable to the key, and
the probe that produced 376,588 was deleted. This entry states the measured total and
refuses the decomposition it cannot support. The V3-era figures that hang off 376,588
(593.8 gwei, 1.82×) are superseded, not corrected: with 395,255 the same formulas
give **565.7 gwei** and **1.73×**.

### What does not change, and the one thing a reader must not conclude

Nothing in §\ref{subsec:evalgas}'s conclusion or in §\ref{sec:zk}'s security argument
depends on which key the number belongs to: the verifier is the same circuit family,
the proof is the same size, and the cost is still dominated by the ingest. What a
reader must not conclude from the correction is that the *deployed* system became 7%
more expensive recently: 306,253 is not "V3 plus a fix", it is a key with one more
public input on a registry that also grew.
