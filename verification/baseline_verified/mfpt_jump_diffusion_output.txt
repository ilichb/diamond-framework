VERDICT
==============================================================================
  - lambda = 0 control: Euler MFPT at dt = 0.005 reproduces
    the analytical value within the discretization bias of the
    Euler scheme (+1.03% for this N = 2000; the dt-convergence
    study reports +4.9% at the same dt with N = 5000, the two
    being statistically indistinguishable at z < 2).
  - As lambda grows, the jump component reduces R and the
    first-passage time to R_0 increases, monotonically.
  - Above a threshold lambda the fraction of paths that reach
    R_0 within T_MAX falls below 1.0; the reported MFPT is
    then a lower bound conditional on absorption.
  - C-03 confirmed: the paper's values (11.2203, 443.7374)
    are the lambda = 0 limit. The MFPT of the process defined
    in Section 2.1 is larger, so the whitewashing bound of
    Section 2.3 is optimistic (lower than the actual time).
