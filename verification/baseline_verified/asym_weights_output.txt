==============================================================================
ASYM WEIGHTS - expected per-pair weight, W vs A_sym
==============================================================================

N = 500, D_BAR = 20, CLIQUE_M = 20, DENSITY_FACTOR = 10

p_out    = D_BAR / (N - 1)                         = 0.040080
p_in     = min(1, DENSITY_FACTOR * p_out)          = 0.400802
d_clique = (CLIQUE_M-1)*p_in + (N-CLIQUE_M)*p_out  = 26.8537
d_bg     = CLIQUE_M*p_in + (N-CLIQUE_M-1)*p_out    = 27.2144

Expected weight of one pair
  Matrix                     intra-clique       background ratio intra/bg
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  W   (raw)                      0.400802         0.040080        10.0000
  A_sym (d_bg = 27.214)          0.014925         0.001473        10.1343
  A_sym (d_bg = 20.000)          0.014925         0.002004         7.4478

Notes:
- d_bg = 27.214 is the expected degree of a background node in the
  H1 graph (clique present). This is the model-consistent denominator.
- d_bg = 20.000 is the null-model degree, cited by section 5.2 as the
  background degree.
- Under either denominator the clique's expected per-pair weight
  exceeds the background's by more than 7, contradicting the
  "cancelled by degree increase" claim.

DENSITY_FACTOR = 10 is the ratio of the underlying edge
probabilities; compare it with the ratios above.

Method: closed form, mean-field expected weights. A_sym divides
by the expected degrees, so the realized weight of one pair
fluctuates with the realized degree sequence.

Part (b) of the task (first-order BBP predictions) is not
implemented; see the docstring for the stop rule applied.
