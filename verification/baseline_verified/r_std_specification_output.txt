==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================
  - R_std is a single nameable statistic. Both canonical scripts
    compute it as R[active].std() (ddof=0) but neither names it.
  - Paper reports 0.0282 (mu/g=5.0, 1 seed) and 0.0424
    (mu/g=1.5, 5 seeds) without declaring operating point or
    seed count. Two regimes, two sample sizes, one symbol.
  - With N=100 at mu/g=1.5 the mean is not 0.0424. The paper's
    cited 0.0424 is a 5-seed realization; its position relative to
    the N=100 CI is printed above.
  - Finding C-14 is textual (R_std never defined) plus numeric
    (operating points and seed counts not declared).
